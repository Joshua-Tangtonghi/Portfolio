Website for the association of my school

Hello everyone, I am delighted to present my annual project: a website for school association management. My goal was to create a user-friendly and efficient platform to facilitate the management of all school association activities and events, while providing a pleasant and intuitive user experience for members. I worked hard to develop a full range of features, including member management, event planning, different role setup and an administrator area.
